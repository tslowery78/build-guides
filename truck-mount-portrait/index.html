Dimensions and limits

Case: 163 × 82.1 mm. Nominal shelf clearance: 5.3 mm. Phone top: 62.3 mm above brow. Assumes the charging ring is centered.

All six post clips now open in the opposite direction. Each side now has two base clips: the inner one opens toward the phone, the outer one toward the dash. The base front strip is 1.2 mm taller to hide the rotated clips. Post clips are 8.8 mm wide behind 10 mm posts; the lower guides fit behind the bottom crossbar. They remain visible when viewed from behind or at an angle. Feed the USB plug through a brace hole once, then tuck the wire into the open clips. The cable passes through the brace instead of looping around its outside; it exits beside the foot, clear of the adhesive pads.

Each brace has a rounded 16 × 12 mm opening with a nominal 3 mm surrounding frame. A modeled 13 × 8 mm plug head passes straight through; your actual plug head is still unmeasured. Both braces remain connected, but their strength has not been validated.

Guide channels are 5.4 mm for an assumed 4.5 mm cable. The displayed cable clears the CAD in a geometric check. Actual grip, bend flexibility and cable size need a physical check.

The screen, shelf and brow are approximate references. Back view hides these to show the cable guides. This is not a driver-eye sightline check.

CAD is one closed solid. Strength, adhesive loading, cable retention, real fit and print supports are not verified. This concept has not been sliced or printed.
