Size & print notes

Each egg: 55 mm tall × 44.6 mm wide. Each animal: 32 mm tall. Both eggs occupy about 97 × 45 mm before a brim or purge tower.

Four colors: pink animals, white-and-black eyes, blue eggs. Animals and eye inserts print separately; both eggs print together with a planned pause to place the finished animals inside. Three batches, with eye assembly and a loading pause.

These are the actual CAD meshes. The shell has a nominal 1.4 mm normal wall, reduced to 0.9 mm at the opening groove, and a 1.2 mm floor. A low rim locates the loose animal. Eye sockets include 0.18 mm fitting clearance; printed fit is untested. No sliced print or print-time estimate yet.
